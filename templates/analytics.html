Statistical methods used on this page
  • Incident rate normalised per 100 resident-days, so months of differing length and occupancy are comparable
  • Box plots (Q1–median–Q3) for wellbeing and fluid intake, showing distribution shape rather than means alone
  • Ordinary least squares trend line on wellbeing — fitted slope = {{ wb_slope }}/month
  • Predictive validity scatter: falls risk score against observed falls per resident
  • Pearson correlation r = {{ fluid_wb_r }} for fluid intake against wellbeing, testing the clinical hypothesis
  • 95% confidence intervals on medication adherence, to convey the uncertainty around each point estimate
  • Incident type × severity heatmap — a two-dimensional cross-tabulation
  • Six-domain radar charts per resident, for multivariate wellbeing comparison
  • Summary statistics (mean, standard deviation, median, min, max) across all wellbeing domains
  • Data completeness: {{ completeness }}%, reported as a data quality measure
